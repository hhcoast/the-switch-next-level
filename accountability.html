Inspect the review criteria

Safety and accessibility

Were risks to pedestrians, wheelchair users and other road users assessed? In a real case, a dated assessment and responsible reviewer should be identified. Evidence here: not available.

Feasibility and budget

Which design alternatives and costs were considered? A credible public response would link to relevant records where lawful. Evidence here: not available.

Alternatives and dissent

Which suggestions were not adopted, and why? Public disagreement should not be erased. Evidence here: illustrative narrative only.

Transfer: independently examine a different case

New fictional case: A library invites suggestions about its opening hours. It later writes: “Additional evening hours have been approved.” A published decision is attached, but no updated timetable or usage data.

What does this establish? What remains open? Formulate your own follow-up before opening the guide.

View the guide

The decision is documented. Whether the extra hours are actually offered, and who benefits, remains unresolved. A useful question might ask for the start date, published timetable and a later study of use. Other reasoned questions are equally valid.

This self-check does not measure lasting transfer. That requires delayed, independent testing.

03 · Citizen follow-up

Which question would improve accountability?

Select a follow-up. The response is generated locally from prewritten examples; nothing is sent anywhere.

Choose a question to examine the quality of the institutional response.

What an accountable reply could contain

Responsibility

Named office and contact route, without exposing individual staff unnecessarily.

Timeline

A specific next review date, or an explanation of why one cannot yet be given.

Verifiability

Accessible records, documented limitations and a clear distinction between intention and completion.

Research & provenance

Scope and limits of this prototype

Civic Return Flow is Stefanie Haslauer's original transition-architecture concept brought into the EU Civic Tech Hackathon 2026. The Switch is the platform collaboratively developed by the hackathon team. The Switch | Next Level is an independent subsequent research and prototype continuation. This statement distinguishes concept provenance from collective implementation; individual contributions beyond this distinction should be verified against project records.

This page demonstrates a design hypothesis: making review, decisions and follow-up legible may support informed participation and organisational learning. Whether it improves trust, accessibility or real-world action requires research and validation.

Development history

01 · Origin

Initial concept

The original idea explored how to make opportunities for public participation easier to find and navigate.

Originator and detailed attribution to be confirmed against project records.

02 · Hackathon

Collaborative development

The hackathon team translated the concept into The Switch, a public-facing discovery experience with five guiding questions: What, When, Where, How and Why.

Original team repository

03 · Next Level

Independent continuation

This separate research prototype investigates comprehension, independent judgement, action and institutional feedback. It does not replace the team project.

Research and safeguards

From clicks to capability

Comprehension

Can people identify the purpose, eligibility and deadline?

Judgement

Can people distinguish an official participation opportunity from a simulated example and choose independently?

Action

Can people locate the verified official participation channel?

Institutional learning

Can recurring access barriers inform better public communication?

From information to visible outcome

This six-stage scenario is fictional. It is not an open consultation, and no answers are collected or sent to a server.

STEP 1 OF 6

Research and safeguards

From clicks to capability — and institutional accountability

Comprehension

Can people understand the purpose, eligibility and deadline?

Judgement

Can people verify official sources and decide independently?

Action

Can people use an official participation channel safely?

Return Flow

Can institutions acknowledge input, explain decisions and show outcomes?

Institutional learning

Can recurring access barriers improve public communication?

Evaluation

Accessibility, transfer, fairness and institutional outcomes require future empirical testing; this demonstration does not establish effectiveness.

Research inspiration includes Paula Klemt's publicly described course on understanding administrative language. No teaching materials or outcomes are reproduced or claimed, and this prototype is not affiliated with that course. AI is optional, not required.
